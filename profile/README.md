## Quick Start with OpenReel Video

1. **Get OpenReel Video** — Grab the official installer from the button above (Windows 10/11 only).
2. **Install** — Run `OpenReelVideo_Setup.exe`. The app opens in under 3 seconds after installation.
3. **Create your profile** — Start with a local profile for everyday use. No phone number required.
4. **Add media folders** — Point OpenReel Video to your clip libraries and project folders.
5. **Pin active reels** — Keep current video projects at the top of your workspace.
6. **Export when ready** — Finish a pass, then move completed items out of Today.
7. **Work normally** — OpenReel Video stays beside your editor while you cut and review.
